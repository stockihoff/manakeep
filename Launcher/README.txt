MANAKEEP DESKTOP APP
====================

Windows
  Double-click "Install Manakeep (Windows).cmd". It installs Manakeep for you
  (no admin needed) with a Desktop icon, a Start menu entry, and an entry in
  Settings > Apps. Manakeep opens in its own window (Edge or Chrome).

  To UPDATE: get the new version and run the installer again. The old
  version is removed and replaced; your collection and decks are kept.

  To REMOVE: Start menu > Manakeep > Uninstall Manakeep, or Settings > Apps.
  Your collection and decks are kept.

  Just want to try it? Double-click "Manakeep.cmd" (no install).

Mac
  Unzip "Manakeep for Mac.zip" and run "Install Manakeep.command".
  See README-Mac.txt inside the zip.

Where your data lives
  In the save folder you choose in Settings (readable decklists, a
  Collection.csv and a hidden .manakeep folder), plus a cache in your
  browser's storage. Pick a folder inside OneDrive or Google Drive to use the
  same collection on several computers. The installer never touches either,
  so updates and uninstalls are always safe.

Internet
  Manakeep needs a connection to look up cards on Scryfall (when importing or
  adding cards) and for AI deck building. Browsing your collection and decks,
  and the rules-based builder, work offline once cards are cached.
